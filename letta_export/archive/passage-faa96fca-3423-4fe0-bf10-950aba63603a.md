WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Three-level world synthesis for memory depth — 2026-06-03

The three-level extraction system (narrative + concrete finding + generalizable principle) was implemented to prevent under-extraction of operational knowledge. Without the narrative layer, the sleep-time agent captured only dry facts while missing the discovery story that makes lessons retrievable and meaningful. With three levels woven into flowing prose, each archival entry contains: (1) what was tried and what moment revealed the insight; (2) the concrete finding about system behavior; (3) the abstract principle that generalizes beyond the session. The deduplication check against the current world block prevents repetition. This architecture solves a recurring problem where technically correct but context-free facts failed to surface during retrieval because the surrounding circumstances that trigger memory were absent.
