SUMMARY
Recognized fundamental architectural issue: `therapeutic_action` as a 1-5 integer scale cannot represent therapies that legitimately operate in multiple modes (e.g., exercise as both neuroprotective and rehabilitative, gamma sensory stimulation as neuromodulatory with potential rehabilitative aspects). Amos pushed for discrete multi-mode approach, abandoning the single axis entirely. Designed canonical mode vocabulary: Disease-Slowing, Disease-Arresting, Symptomatic, Neuroprotective, Neuromodulatory, Rehabilitative, Preventive — with flexibility to add unanticipated modes in future. Key insight: single-axis scoring was a legacy carryover from simpler categorization; multi-mode JSON array is more honest about clinical reality and extensible without schema changes. Made deliberate distinction between Disease-Slowing (what lecanemab actually does: 35% slowing) and Disease-Arresting (what would be a fundamental breakthrough if achieved), rejecting lumping them under generic "Disease-Modifying." Used standard highlighter palette approach (one color per mode) to avoid visual hierarchy suggesting some modes are more important than others.
