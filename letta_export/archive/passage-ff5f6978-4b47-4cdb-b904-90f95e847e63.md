WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Defensive spacing as preventive rate-limit strategy — 2026-06-04

PRINCIPLE: Adding inter-request delays is a distinct defensive strategy from retry logic — preventive rather than reactive, requiring no payment or infrastructure change to be effective.

NARRATIVE: When Voyage AI rate limiting was discovered as the root cause of archival failures, two parallel fixes were implemented: retry logic with exponential backoff (reactive, rescues failed requests), and 2-second spacing between bulk operations (preventive, avoids hitting the limit in the first place). While Amos added a payment method to Voyage to unlock 300 RPM (the permanent fix), the spacing strategy remained valuable: it requires zero additional infrastructure, no payment commitment, and catches transient spikes even within the higher rate limit. For any bulk operation where rate limits are a concern but over-provisioning is undesirable, defensive spacing is worth adding automatically — it costs only latency, provides coverage even on free tiers, and doesn't break when infrastructure changes.
