SUMMARY
Amos walked through the existing post formats. For regular events, the format is: `[Event title] - at Date & Time: [event date] [event time] - Venue: [event venue] - [description]` followed by the detail page URL. An example: "K-Pop Demon Hunters Party - at Grand - Date & Time: Saturday, May 30, 2026 2:00pm - 4:00pm - Venue: Grand Street Branch - Program Room,Study Room, Hoboken, NJ - Dance, craft, and watch as we celebrate AAPI Month with a K-Pop Demon Hunters Party!" followed by the event detail URL.

Gallery exhibitions are posted on a less frequent schedule (twice a month), up to 3 days in advance, also avoiding reposts and past events. Daimon suggested a cleaner exhibit format that proved more appealing to Amos than the original Tasklet version. The agreed format is: `[Exhibit title] \n\n Venue: [venue] \n [exhibit type/duration] \n [description] \n More info: [URL] \n\n [hashtags]` — an example being "Infinity Climber \n\n Venue: Liberty Science Ctr \n Permanent exhibit \n Suspended climbing play space. Measures 26 feet wide, 24 feet deep, and 19 feet tall, with 19 miles of protective mesh wire. \n More info: https://lsc.org/explore/exhibitions/infinity-climber \n\n #HobokenArts #JerseyCity #LocalCulture #Exhibition"

The spreadsheet contains columns: Detail URL, Event Date, Event Time, Venue, Title, Description, Image URL, Image Type, Posted At, Scraped Date.
