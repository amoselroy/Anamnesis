WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-07 — Three-level audience explanation as differentiation — 2026-06-07

PRINCIPLE: Same content explained at three different epistemic levels (clinical rigor, informed non-specialist, layperson simplification) creates a publicly differentiable resource and serves all audiences simultaneously.

NARRATIVE: When designing Braindexer (an Alzheimer's therapy tracking platform for physicians and patients), the insight emerged that no existing resource bridges the gap between dense academic literature (inaccessible to most patients) and advocacy-driven information (often optimistic without rigor). A three-tier approach — each therapy entry rendered at clinical-detail level, informed-individual level (lay-technical middle ground), and simplified-analogy level — serves all audiences from the same source material while making the platform itself a trusted bridge between professional and public understanding. This approach applies to any domain where multiple stakeholder types need the same information but at different depths: medical guidance, technical specifications, research findings, or policy analysis.
