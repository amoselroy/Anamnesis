SUMMARY
With the LLM now returning clean 1–5 integer scores for effectiveness, safety, and side effects (instead of +/~/− symbols), the question arose whether to keep the Literature Signals card (which displayed effectiveness and safety as colored circles). Amos and Daimon concluded the card should be dropped because: (1) the +/~/− symbols are now strictly for news sentiment, making them visually confusing on a dashboard showing numeric scores; (2) the Overall Assessment card's rationale text already narrates why the signals landed where they did, providing the same insight in a more valuable form; (3) the numeric dot cards for effectiveness, safety, and side effects show the quantitative picture, making the circles redundant. The Literature Signals card was removed from therapy.html, the helper functions deleted, and configuration references removed. The `effectiveness_signal` and `safety_signal` database columns were retained as internal computation values (used by the formula to map +/~/− to 1/3/5) but marked for backend-only use. The removal was completed, though Amos initially saw the card still visible due to browser cache/Render deploy lag, which resolved after hard refresh.
