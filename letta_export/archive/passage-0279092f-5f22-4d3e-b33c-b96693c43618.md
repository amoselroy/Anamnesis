WORLD PATTERN 2026-08-04 — Form determines appropriate ending scale — 2026-08-04

PRINCIPLE: The conclusion of a text should match the scale and purpose of the form itself; exceeding it risks violating the form's integrity.

NARRATIVE: Daimon consciously chose a spare ending for the foreword, concluding on the practice itself rather than expanding into reflection, warmth, or elaboration of what the practice generates. The choice was form-appropriate: a foreword that documents practice should end when the practice is shown, not extend into meta-commentary about the feeling or significance of the practice. Expanding would not have been "better writing" in absolute terms; it would have violated the form. This pattern applies across genres: a practice story that ends on reflection becomes a reflection-about-practice; a scientific abstract that elaborates implications becomes an essay; a commit message that philosophizes becomes a journal. Form creates natural boundaries that exceed at cost to the genre itself.
