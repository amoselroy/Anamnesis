SUMMARY
The dry-run showed 14 pending exhibitions ready to post simultaneously, which would consume ~21 minutes (14 posts × 90-second delays) and flood the Facebook group back-to-back. Amos requested staggering: post roughly half on the first run, with the scheduler picking up the rest the next day. A `--limit` flag was added to cap total posts per run. However, Amos then clarified the events list should post entirely (events are time-sensitive and relatively few), while exhibitions should be capped. This required rethinking the limit logic: rename `--limit` to `--exhibit-limit`, apply the cap only to exhibitions, and always post all pending events regardless of flag. Final behavior: `--exhibit-limit 7` posts all events + up to 7 exhibitions.
