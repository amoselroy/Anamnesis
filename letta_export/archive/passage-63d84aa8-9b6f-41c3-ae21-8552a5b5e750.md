WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-22 — Dual-axis status modeling for approval history preservation — 2026-06-22

PRINCIPLE: When an entity can transition between distinct states (approved→withdrawn, trials-running→discontinued), use two separate orthogonal axes rather than a single state field, and ensure historical metadata is preserved even when current status changes.

NARRATIVE: Initial modeling treated therapy status as a single field ("active", "discontinued"). But Aduhelm revealed the limitation: FDA-approved 2021, withdrawn from market 2024. A single "withdrawn" flag discards the historical approval fact. Solution: two orthogonal axes — `status` (active/discontinued/withdrawn) and `approved_by` array with `{agency, year, withdrawn_year}`. This preserves: (1) what happened historically, (2) what the current state is, (3) both are rendered simultaneously to the user. The insight generalizes: whenever an entity has undergone distinct lifecycle transitions (publish→unpublish, approve→revoke, activate→disable), consider whether two independent axes (current state + historical record) communicate more completely than a single state field, and whether collapsing them loses important information.
