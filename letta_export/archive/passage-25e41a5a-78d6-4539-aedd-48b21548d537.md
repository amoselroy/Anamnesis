WORLD PATTERN 2026-08-06 — Generic SiteHandler Extension as Alternative to Custom Handlers — 2026-08-06

PRINCIPLE: When discovering that a pattern applies to one source initially, implementing it as an extensible SiteHandler field is preferable to custom handler code, because it remains testable, reusable, and doesn't accumulate venue-specific branches.

NARRATIVE: Both `default_time` and `companion_url` were initially conceptualized as LSC-specific problems — the scraper's handler routing was about to gain LSC-custom logic. Amos clarified that LSC doesn't have a unique calendar structure; they have specific event series pages that happen to exhibit these patterns. Recognizing the pattern as generic (implicit times, companion enrichment) rather than LSC-specific meant implementing it at the SiteHandler level, where it applies regardless of which venue uses it. This preserves the architecture's principle that custom handlers are for platform-specific rendering (Eventbrite JavaScript, ticketing portal redirects), not for source-specific data patterns. The lesson: when you find yourself reaching for a custom handler to solve a problem for one source, pause and ask whether the pattern is actually source-specific or just happens to appear there first.
