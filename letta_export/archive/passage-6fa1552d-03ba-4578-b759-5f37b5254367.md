WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-06 — Schema extractability through deliberate isolation from inception — 2026-06-07

PRINCIPLE: Separate new systems at the data layer from their inception using isolated schemas and independent environment variables, enabling clean extraction to independent infrastructure later without architectural untangling.

NARRATIVE: When Braindexer was designed to share Neon with MemShepherd, the instinct was to consolidate. Instead, Amos insisted on deliberate separation: its own `braindexer` schema, its own `BRAINDEXER_DB_URI` environment variable, no cross-schema dependencies. This costs nothing at inception but makes future independent deployment trivial — when Braindexer grows beyond the initial shared Neon instance, migration is a straightforward data dump and env var repoint, with zero entanglement to unpack. The principle generalizes: whenever a new system shares infrastructure with existing systems (database, container, queue), design the isolation boundaries as if eventual independence is expected. This is the opposite of the common pattern of monolithic databases and shared infrastructure that become impossible to separate.
