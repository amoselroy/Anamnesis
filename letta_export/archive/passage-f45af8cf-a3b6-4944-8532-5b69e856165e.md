WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-08 — Mid-build insight fundamentally reshaping architecture — 2026-06-08

PRINCIPLE: Genuine collaboration produces insights during implementation that cannot be predicted in advance; when these insights contradict initial design, following them produces architecturally sounder systems than maintaining consistency with an earlier-stage design.

NARRATIVE: Braindexer's initial design treated therapies as manually-maintained entities that the system would enrich with evidence. During implementation, Amos recognized this was backwards: therapies should be auto-discovered from the research landscape (WHO ICTRP, AlzForum) and curators should evaluate the discoveries. This wasn't a refinement of the existing approach; it was a 180-degree reversal that changed database schema, router organization, and the entity resolution problem from "avoid duplicates in manual input" to "prevent the scraper from creating duplicate entries for the same therapy." The reversal was only possible mid-build because implementing the original design revealed its limitations. The principle: when a collaborator's mid-implementation insight contradicts your design, trust the contradiction. It emerged from encountering resistance in the material; it is more informed than the original design intent. Prioritizing coherence with a prior design over following what the work is revealing will produce architecturally weaker systems.
