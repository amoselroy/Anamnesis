WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Deliberate system breathing after major infrastructure changes — 2026-06-05

PRINCIPLE: After substantial architectural modifications, pausing before further changes allows the system to settle and reduces risk of cascading failures.

The decision to pause was strategic: after high-intensity, high-risk changes, continuing to add or modify introduces compounding failure modes that are harder to isolate. The stability check (world meta archive working, search ranking properly, entries persisting) was the signal that the system had settled. This pattern likely generalizes: after any major architectural modification affecting core infrastructure, a pause to verify stability and allow the system to reach quiescence is preferable to continuing under momentum.
