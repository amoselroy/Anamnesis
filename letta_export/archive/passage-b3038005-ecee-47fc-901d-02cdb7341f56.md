SUMMARY
Amos reported HTTP 500 errors when running the new "Update News" button, then discovered that no articles were being inserted despite the scraper finding content. Investigation revealed a cascade of two separate issues. First, the `update_news` endpoint in `routers/therapies.py` was calling `log.info()` without importing the logging module, causing a `NameError`. Second and more subtle, the root cause of zero insertions was a `[:10]` slice in `scrape_google_news()` that was silently filtering out all viable articles: Google News RSS returns pharmaceutical trade press and general Alzheimer's articles in the first 10 slots (NeurologyLive, Biogen, etc.), which were all being rejected by the outlet whitelist filter. The correct outlets (ScienceDaily, EurekAlert, Medical News Today) only appeared in slots 11+. After removing the slice to scan all 100 returned items, the function correctly identified 19 passing articles. Both fixes were applied: adding `import logging` and removing the `[:10]` slice. The button was also refactored into a "Manage News" modal that consolidated viewing, deleting, and fetching articles into a single UI panel with a delete endpoint (`DELETE /therapies/sources/{id}`). The lesson: silent filtering (the slice) combined with filtering (the whitelist) had created a confluence where *every* potential article was rejected before insertion, with no error surfaced to the user.
