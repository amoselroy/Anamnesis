Clicking "Next" (exact match) followed by optional step-2 confirmation ("Post"/"Share"/"Done"/"Publish") resolves the submission flow. Fix: use `get_by_role("button", name="Next", exact=True)` for page post submission. This fix was applied 2026-05-28 23:28 and verified working 2026-05-29 — posts now successfully appear on Team page (61586443622077) feed.
