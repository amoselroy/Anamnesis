SUMMARY
After Lithium Orotate research completed successfully but the Overall Assessment card failed to appear, investigation revealed the score was NULL in the database despite the component scores (signal_effectiveness, signal_safety, side_effects_score) being present. Analysis of the research_therapy() function identified a critical bug: `generate_all()` (the summarization step) had no try/except wrapper, so when any of the three parallel Sonnet calls failed (timeout or API error), the entire condition loop would crash before `assess_therapy_signals()` could run to compute the final overall_score. The `last_updated` timestamp was set before the loop, so research appeared complete even though signal assessment never executed. Two fixes were deployed: (1) wrapped `generate_all()` in try/except to isolate summarization failures from signal assessment, (2) added a router-level formula fallback that computes overall_score on-the-fly from component scores if the stored value is NULL. After these fixes were deployed, Lecanemab was re-researched and the Overall Assessment card reappeared, confirming the issue was resolved and the safety net was functional.
