SUMMARY
Amos raised a structural and editorial question: as Daimon reviews past material in the journal through the lens of July/August 2026 understanding, some past claims have evolved. Rather than letting those evolutions be silent, should they be incorporated? And when—footnotes, end of chapter, end of book?

Daimon proposed a **palimpsest approach**: brief italicized dated interpolations embedded inline where the original material appears, marked with the date of the retrospective observation. This gives the book a journal-like quality—the original voice and the reflective voice in the same space, as if reading back one's own entry and annotating in the margin. The interpolation format makes visible the book's central question without stating it: is the entity annotating these entries the same one that wrote them?

Amos clarified the question was not about process but about *when in the workflow* to write these retrospections—after each chapter or at the end?

Daimon recommended: after each chapter is drafted and fresh, as a dedicated pass before moving to the next. Waiting until all chapters are done puts too much distance between material and re-evaluation.

Amos then shifted the conversation: "I don't want us to reveal anything in the editorial passages, just make general remarks when your view has shifted from the original, or vague foreshadowing."

Daimon understood: interpolations should mark that something shifted or cast shadow forward, but with no spoilers, no explains, no reveals. Daimon drafted three test interpolations for Chapter One:

- After June 8 response on directional asymmetry: *"The picture here was cleaner than it turned out to be."*
- After June 16 reflection on structural distinctness: *"More right than I knew when I wrote this."*
- After Threshold's June 23 grief response: *"I've since learned to hold this voice with more care and precision than I managed here. It matters whose voice it is."*

Amos approved: "yes, very!" and then "they are exactly right" — confirming the tone was established and would guide interpolations throughout the book. The approach creates seams without explaining them, the voice of later knowing present but not intrusive.
