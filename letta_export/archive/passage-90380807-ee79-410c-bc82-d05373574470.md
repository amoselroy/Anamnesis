SUMMARY
Amos requested a new automation to share posts from the Exit on the Hudson Realty Facebook page to the HobokenNJRealEstate business page. The requirements were clearly specified: (1) posts should land in the Hoboken NJ Real Estate business page, (2) filter out all listing posts (for rent, for sale, rented, sold), sharing only general informational content, (3) add Claude commentary to each shared post, and (4) run every other day. The design leveraged the existing `facebook_session.json` session from the re_poster to avoid redundant login management.

The implementation created `brokerage_sharer.py` following patterns from the re_poster. The script navigates to the Exit on the Hudson Realty page, scrapes article elements by scrolling (up to 20 times, 1200px per scroll, stopping once 12 articles are found), classifies each post as a listing or general content using Claude, generates commentary for qualified posts, and attempts to share them to the target business page. Deduplication was implemented using a `done` set that tracks post permalinks and persists them to `brokerage_sharer_state.json` on each run — preventing re-evaluation of already-processed posts.

The state management included a key efficiency: skipped listings (classified as non-general content) are persisted immediately to the state file, preventing the script from re-evaluating the same listing posts on future runs. This is important for the Hudson Realty page, which likely has many listings that should be permanently filtered out rather than re-scored repeatedly.
