SUMMARY
Completed review of Donepezil's Alzheimer's Disease-specific clinical content (58 papers, Cochrane reviews, 2026 AChEI network meta-analysis, comparison table with other cholinesterase inhibitors). Content accuracy verified as strong, with appropriate limitations sections flagging AD2000 52-week endpoint, publication bias, and missing biomarker stratification. However, discovered two critical score misalignments: (1) Mode of Action labeled "Primarily Arresting" directly contradicts the clinical text which correctly states donepezil is symptomatic and doesn't modify underlying pathology; (2) Overall Assessment scored "Promising" is inappropriate for a 1996 FDA-approved standard-of-care drug and should be "Established" or "Standard of Care." Both scores generated by `assess_therapy` model. Identified root cause: the 1-5 integer scale for `therapeutic_action` was fundamentally misaligned with the clinical reality of AD interventions, collapsing symptom compensation, neuroprotection, disease-modification, and rehabilitation into a single axis that cannot represent multi-modal therapies honestly.
