SUMMARY
Amos confirmed that despite the trailing space hashtag fix and dialog-scoped Post button targeting in the previous session, posts submitted to the Team page (61586443622077) were still not appearing. The script logged "Post submitted" for multiple articles, but inspection of the Team page showed no new posts. This persistent gap between logged success and actual absence motivated a deeper investigation into what was happening at the Post submission step.

**Business Suite Investigation:**
I proposed switching the automation to use Meta Business Suite (`business.facebook.com`) targeting the public page (100063973542185) directly, bypassing the account-switcher complexity and the Team page entirely. The hypothesis was that Business Suite provides a more reliable admin interface. I wrote `debug_biz_post.py` to test whether navigating to `business.facebook.com/latest/home/?asset_id=100063973542185` would expose a proper "Create post" button for the public page. Amos ran the debug and reported that Business Suite did not work for that page — the page was not connected to a Business Portfolio, so the Business Suite interface was inaccessible.

**Post Click Observation:**
Since Business Suite was ruled out and the Team page remained the accessible automation target, I shifted to directly observing what happens when the Post button is clicked. I wrote `debug_post_watch.py` to take screenshots every second after the Post click, capturing the modal state, any toasts, errors, or other UI responses. The script used a minimal test post (no hashtags, no URL) to rule out autocomplete complications. Amos ran the script and reported that the post did not submit, and provided a screenshot from the final step showing the modal state at the time.

**Root Cause Discovery:**
By analyzing the screenshots captured during the post-click sequence, I discovered the actual problem: **the Create post modal on a Facebook Page uses "Next" as the submit button, not "Post"**. The script's button selector was matching "Add to your post" — a text string that contains the word "post" but is actually the attachment menu trigger. When the script clicked that element, it opened the attachment sub-menu instead of submitting the post. The modal remained open, and no post was created. The logs showed "Post submitted" because the script's exception handling for the click was treating any successful click as success, regardless of whether it submitted the actual post.

This explained the entire mystery: the script had been clicking the wrong button (the attachment menu trigger) while believing it was submitting. The modal would open, text would be typed, but the submission button was never clicked — only the attachment menu was opened instead.

**Fix and Resolution:**
The solution is a single change: replace the "Post" button selector with "Next" and modify the submission flow to handle the multi-step modal (Facebook Pages require a "Next" step, which typically leads to a final confirmation or additional options). I prepared to apply this fix to `re_poster.py`, identified it as a durable one-line change, and prepared to compact the session after applying it.
