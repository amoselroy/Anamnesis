**Selector and Interaction Debugging:**
Over multiple iterations, we discovered and fixed several Playwright selector and interaction issues:

1. **Account switching and page navigation:** The script successfully switched to the Team page but landed on the Facebook feed rather than the page itself. Fixed by explicitly navigating to the Facebook page URL after switching rather than relying on the landing URL.

2. **Composer location:** The "Share a thought..." button at the top of the page opened a **Story/Note composer** (limited to 60 characters, 24-hour visibility), not the regular post composer. The actual post composer ("What's on your mind?") was located ~700px down the page in the feed area. Fixed by adding scroll logic before opening the composer.

3. **Hashtag dropdown blocking the Post button:** When the script typed hashtags (e.g., `#NJRealEstate`), Facebook's autocomplete dropdown appeared over the Post button, making the button unclickable. Initial attempts to dismiss the dropdown with `Escape` closed the entire modal, not just the dropdown. Eventually fixed by adding a trailing space to the hashtags string to finalize them before looking for the Post button, eliminating the need for explicit dropdown dismissal.

4. **Post button scope:** The Post button selector was initially too broad, potentially matching buttons elsewhere on the page. Fixed by scoping the button search to within the dialog element only.

5. **Dialog header interactions:** An intermediate fix that attempted to click the dialog header to dismiss the dropdown caused a "dancing" effect where the script repeatedly clicked and re-focused elements. Abandoned in favor of the trailing-space fix.
