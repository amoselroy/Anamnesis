SUMMARY
Implemented two admin console UI improvements requested by Amos: adding `last_updated` date to the SUMMARIES column (showing "✓ Generated [date]" or "None") and converting the Self-Admin Notes field from a single-line input to a multiline textarea with 3 rows. Investigated why semantic vector search was failing for queries like "light," "mineral," and "acid" despite successfully returning results for "sauna" and "stimulation." Analysis revealed that the 0.82 cosine distance threshold was too strict for name-only embeddings (mechanism and summary fields were NULL when initial embeddings were generated), filtering out valid semantic neighbors. The fix involved two components: (1) loosening the threshold from 0.82 to 0.92 to allow more semantic flexibility, then to 0.87 as the final tuned value, and (2) a more fundamental improvement: modifying `embed_therapy()` to include therapy aliases in the embedding text, so queries for "lithium" would match "Lithium Orotate" and "light" would match therapies with photobiomodulation-related aliases. Added two new admin endpoints—`POST /therapies/re-embed-all` (re-embeds all active therapies without re-running research or summarization) and `POST /therapies/{slug}/re-embed` (re-embeds a single therapy)—with corresponding admin UI buttons to trigger re-embedding. This design recognizes that embeddings can be regenerated independently from the expensive research and summarization steps. After Amos ran Research & Summarize on all 5 therapies and hit "Re-embed All," all embeddings were refreshed with both rich content (mechanism + summary_informed) and aliases, making the 0.87 threshold work meaningfully for semantic filtering.
