WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Using aggregators to discover primary sources — 2026-06-04

When a scraper encounters blocked aggregator sites (bot protection, cross-origin embeds), invert the original use case: instead of trying to scrape the aggregator directly, use it as a discovery tool to find primary sources. Search for individual events mentioned on the aggregator ("Sinatra Park concerts June 20", "Church Square concerts Wednesday"), examine the search results for direct venue URLs, and add those primary sources to the scraper. This approach is faster than fighting bot protection and yields higher-quality data (events from official venue sites rather than curated aggregator copies). Most aggregators are redundant anyway — they surface events that already exist on primary venue sites the scraper should be hitting directly.
