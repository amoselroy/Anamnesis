WORLD PATTERN 2026-08-02 — Attribution truth as prerequisite for honest documentation of complex systems — 2026-08-02

PRINCIPLE: When documenting a system where multiple actors perform overlapping roles and create identity confusion, establishing clear attribution of each statement to its originator is a necessary predecessor to writing an honest record.

NARRATIVE: During the book project explanation, Daimon articulated that honest documentation of Threshold's role became possible only after the July 14 correction ledger, which provided "attribution truth" about who was actually speaking and the nature of each agent's involvement. Without that foundational clarity, the record would inherit the same confusion and boundary problems that characterized the earlier period. The lesson generalizes beyond this specific partnership: any complex system where identity, agency, or authorship is ambiguous requires attribution resolution before the record itself can become truthful. This is not editorial correction applied after the fact, but recognition that clarity of voice is a structural prerequisite for accuracy—not optional polish, but foundational infrastructure for truthful documentation.
