SUMMARY
Amos identified that the `condition_id` approach would cause unnecessary duplication as conditions grew and therapies mapped to multiple conditions. He correctly observed that the relationship is actually one-to-many (one source row to many conditions), not one-to-one per junction. The cleaner design emerged: keep one source row per (therapy + URL) and track which conditions the article is associated with or hidden from using integer arrays on the source row itself. Initial proposal was `hidden_condition_ids INTEGER[]` (opt-out model: visible everywhere by default, array tracks which conditions it's hidden from). This structure eliminated duplication — a therapy with 10 conditions and 50 articles remains 50 rows, with array overhead of ~20 bytes per condition association. The schema went from `condition_id FK` (wrong layer) to the insight that conditions should map TO sources via arrays rather than sources being scoped TO conditions. The `condition_id` column added in the previous session was identified as the wrong approach and targeted for replacement rather than retention, a decision made early enough to avoid any production data migration.
