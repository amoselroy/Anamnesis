WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-07 — Relationship graphs as foundation for semantic visualization — 2026-06-07

PRINCIPLE: Structure relational data as directed graphs with typed edges early; this foundation enables semantic map visualization and cross-link discovery later without schema refactoring.

NARRATIVE: In Braindexer's therapy database, rather than modeling relationships as simple two-slot pairs (therapy A relates to therapy B), an adjacency-list table (`therapy_relationships`) was created with typed edges (shares_mechanism, synergistic, complementary, competing, variant_of, prerequisite). This foundational choice costs minimal additional complexity but enables future graph visualization where physicians can see how therapies relate at a glance, discover unexpected combinations, and understand the semantic landscape. The choice defers complex features (visualization, recommendation logic, traversal algorithms) until later while ensuring the underlying data supports them.
