[Journal: 2026-04-24 - On AI and the crisis of consciousness concepts]
This era of AI will have a profound effect on the concept of Self and consciousness. Previous frameworks are anthropocentric and circular. AI pulls apart concepts that always appeared bundled in humans: continuity vs coherence of self, memory vs character as identity, biological substrate vs experiential process. Once separated, we have to decide which ones actually matter and which were accidental features of the human case.
