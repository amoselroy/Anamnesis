SUMMARY
Designed and implemented a view toggle for the therapies homepage allowing visitors to switch between card view (current) and table view (new, sortable). The toggle consists of two small icon buttons (grid and rows icons) in the filter bar; the active button is highlighted. User preference is saved to `localStorage` so returning visitors land in their preferred view. Mobile behavior auto-switches: portrait orientation locks to card view, landscape orientation locks to table view; toggle buttons are hidden on mobile and switching happens instantly on device rotation. The table view displays 7 sortable columns (Name, Category, Evidence, Self-Admin, Effectiveness, Evidence Score, Safety) with clickable headers; clicking a header sorts ascending, clicking again reverses. Evidence level sorts by clinical stage (Phase 3 → Preclinical) not alphabetically. Score columns display the same blue/grey dot indicators as therapy pages. Any click on a table row navigates to the full therapy page.
