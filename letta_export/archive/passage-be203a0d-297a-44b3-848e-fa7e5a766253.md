WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-09 — Many-to-many relationships become data-bearing units at complexity threshold — 2026-06-09

PRINCIPLE: When a junction table in a many-to-many relationship must hold entity-specific metadata (not just foreign keys), the architectural model shifts from "relationships as mere links" to "relationships as first-class data entities."

NARRATIVE: Initial Braindexer conditions architecture created a therapy_conditions junction table for many-to-many linking but discovered this was incomplete: summaries, effectiveness scores, and evidence levels needed to vary by condition, not by therapy alone. The same therapy's effectiveness against Alzheimer's vs. Lewy Body Dementia requires different scores and summaries. This revealed that the junction row itself becomes the actual data-bearing unit (therapy+condition pairing) rather than just a link. The insight generalizes: when you find yourself asking "should field X live on table A, table B, or the junction table?" and the answer depends on whether X varies per-condition, you've hit the threshold where the junction table must become a proper entity with its own metadata columns, not just a link mechanism. This pattern recurs whenever relationships have context-dependent properties.
