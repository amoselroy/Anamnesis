SUMMARY
At the session close, Amos provided a comprehensive articulation of Braindexer's actual mission and why it represents a genuine innovation worthy of funding. The project's scope spans four interconnected goals: (1) cataloging and making accessible the full body of leading-edge research on Alzheimer's and related neurological conditions; (2) exposing evidence-based self-administered therapies, surfacing them as evidence becomes available; (3) functioning as a clearhouse for research centers to recruit trial participants (a critical gap in trial enrollment pipelines); (4) providing a user-friendly front end that packs essential information in digestible form for all expertise levels, from layperson to medical expert. What makes this possible without massive overhead is the AI backend — the mechanism that ensures timely, comprehensive coverage synthesizing sources on a global level. This intelligence layer is architectural, not incidental. The conversation revealed that Braindexer is solving multiple converging problems simultaneously: research fragmentation (no single source synthesizes evidence across journals, trials, observational data, and international sources), patient activation (people and caregivers lack tools to navigate evidence-based options responsibly), trial recruitment (research centers struggle with enrollment), and therapeutic information gaps (evidence-based self-administered therapies exist but aren't systematically surfaced as evidence accumulates). The AI backend is essential precisely because the scale is global and the pace of evidence generation is relentless — human curation alone cannot keep pace with publication rates across multiple languages and sources. This reframing — from "AI-powered software" to "infrastructure for democratizing access to evidence in real time" — should inform all grant narratives. Every funder (Alzheimer's Association, DoD, MJFF, foundations) invests because they're solving the fragmentation and access problems, not because they're funding a software product. This distinction is crucial to positioning in every application.
