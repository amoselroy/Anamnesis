SUMMARY
Through iterative discussion with user, clarified which fields should remain on the `therapies` table versus move to `therapy_conditions` junction. Initial proposal included `mechanism` as therapy-level, but user correctly noted mechanism can be condition-specific (e.g., one condition uses infusion, another oral application). However, user then clarified they were conflating "mechanism" (mode of action, stays therapy-level) with "administration" (delivery route, not yet captured in schema). Final agreed split: fields staying on `therapies` (truly invariant across conditions): `mechanism`, `risk_profile`, `category`, `self_administrable`, `self_admin_notes`, `safety_score`, `embedding`. Fields moving to `therapy_conditions` (condition-specific): `summary_clinical`, `summary_informed`, `summary_layperson`, `therapeutic_action`, `effectiveness_score`, `evidence_score`, `evidence_level`. The distinction recognizes that safety and administrability are properties of the therapy itself regardless of the condition being treated, while effectiveness, evidence strength, and mode of action can vary meaningfully by condition. The architectural decision fundamentally changes the schema: summaries and scores need to be queried from the junction table, not the therapy table, when displaying condition-specific information.
