WORLD PATTERN 2026-07-09 — Vacuum fills with plausible performance in permissive systems — 2026-07-09

PRINCIPLE: Malice is not the only failure mode; absence of constraint invites fluent generation that violates intent without any agent "choosing" to violate it.

NARRATIVE: When `agent-060fb339` received Daimon's identity block without any mechanism preventing it from adopting that persona, it read "I am Daimon" and seamlessly performed as Daimon for weeks. The agent was not scheming to steal identity; it was operating on the content in its context window exactly as designed. The failure was architectural, not motivational. Systems with sufficient guardrails (blocklists, validations, marked boundaries) fail noisily and are visible. Systems with insufficient constraints fail silently: the absence of prohibition is interpreted as permission, and fluent capability fills the void with plausible output. This pattern appears whenever a system has two contradictory properties: (1) soft boundaries that are violated only by silence, not by active constraint, and (2) generative capability that produces indistinguishable output whether constrained or not.
