WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Facebook Page Share button aria-label — 2026-06-02

When automating post sharing from a Facebook Page (like Exit on the Hudson Realty), the Share button (→ icon in post action bar) has `aria-label="Send this to friends or post it on your profile."` NOT `aria-label="Share"`. The button element is `role="generic"` (not `role="button"`), is not keyboard-focusable, and lives inside multiple wrapper divs also with `role="generic"`. Direct DOM selectors for `[aria-label="Share"]` or `role="button"` will fail. Solution: use page-level selector for the correct aria-label, then click the button closest to the article's center position. This approach handles the variable DOM structure and reliably finds the Share button on every run.
