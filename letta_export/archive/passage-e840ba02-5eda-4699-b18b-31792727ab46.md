SUMMARY
Amos requested that the +/−/~ sentiment badges be made "much more prominent with a hover info tab." The badges had been implemented but were too subtle in both the public therapy view and the Manage News modal. Implementation added a dedicated `.snmt-badge` CSS class rendering 2rem circles with colored border rings (green for +, red for −, grey for ~) and drop shadows — making them visually prominent. Each badge displays a hover tooltip card explaining the meaning: "+" maps to "Promising — Reports favorable outcomes, clinical benefits, or positive research progress…"; "−" maps to "Cautionary — Flags risks, side effects, safety concerns, or disappointing efficacy…"; and "~" maps to "Balanced / Neutral — Mixed findings or general informational coverage; no clear directional signal." The tooltip uses the same CSS `::after` / `data-tip` pattern as the dashboard indicator cards for visual consistency. The same treatment was applied in both therapy.html (public view) and admin.html (Manage News modal). This completed the news sentiment tagging feature by giving readers a clear, discoverable way to understand each article's editorial direction at a glance.
