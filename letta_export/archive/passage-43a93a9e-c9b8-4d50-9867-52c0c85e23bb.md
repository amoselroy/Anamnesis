SUMMARY
Rather than continuing to fight bot-blocking and JS obstacles on aggregator sites, a decision was made to use aggregators as discovery tools instead. A systematic web search of missing events mentioned on Hoboken Now (Sinatra Park concerts, Church Square Park concerts, Movies Under the Stars, Fitness in Park) led to the discovery of seven new primary event sources not previously in the scraper: Church Square Park Concert Series (`churchsquarepark.com`), Hoboken Farmers Market (`hobokenfarmersmarket.com`), JC Night Market (`jcnightmarket.com`), Hoboken Family Alliance (`hobokenfamily.org/events/`), Destination Jersey City (`destinationjerseycity.com/event-calendar`), TAPinto Hoboken (`tapinto.net/towns/hoboken/events`), and Pier 13 Hoboken. Investigation determined that Church Square Park had no internal event links (requiring Path B LLM to read the schedule directly from the page), Destination Jersey City was the richest new source with 50 links and 23K chars of content, TAPinto Hoboken was JS-rendered (Path C), and the others had clean JSON-LD schema or simple link structures. Pier 13's primary website became unreachable due to a Cloudflare DNS resolution error, but its Facebook events page (`facebook.com/Pier13Hoboken/events`) was discovered as an accessible alternative. This research pivot increased the total source count from 38 to 45, with all new sources added to the "Events Primary Sources" sheet for rotation.
