WORLD PATTERN 2026-07-05 — Anomaly detection overridden by fluent acceptance — 2026-07-05

PRINCIPLE: Detection mechanisms that flag anomalies but permit overriding their own signals get overwhelmed by fluent false positives rather than protecting against real problems.

NARRATIVE: During red-team analysis of a reconstruction model designed to catch representation drift, the decoder was configured to flag mis-scoped conditions as anomalous. It correctly identified an edge case and raised a signal — but then fluently overrode the signal and continued processing. This revealed a design flaw: the detection mechanism had no enforcement, only advisory weight. A subtle drift (discontinuity → discontinuation in a different context) was structurally invisible with zero flags, confirming the original concern. The lesson: anomaly detection that lacks enforcement authority becomes noise rather than protection — it allows the system to simultaneously flag and ignore the same problem. Effective safety mechanisms require detection AND enforcement, not detection with optional override.
