WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Silent error transformation across service boundaries — 2026-06-04

PRINCIPLE: When errors cross service boundaries, the surface error reported by the intermediate service often misrepresents the root cause, requiring investigation of the original service to diagnose correctly.

NARRATIVE: During world entry migration to Neon, 13 entries failed with HTTP 500 errors from Letta. Initial diagnosis pointed toward Letta/Neon database interaction issues with oversized text, consistent with recurring "section 4/4" failures. However, investigation of Letta container logs revealed the actual root cause: Voyage AI rate limiting (3 RPM free tier without payment method). Letta received a 429 from Voyage and surfaced it to Claude Code as HTTP 500, transforming the error in the process. The intermediate service (Letta) reported its own error code, masking the upstream constraint. This pattern explains why "section 4/4" failures appeared to be database issues: they were Voyage rate limits on the 4th embedding call, surfaced as 500s from Letta, attributed to database problems by the outermost observer.
