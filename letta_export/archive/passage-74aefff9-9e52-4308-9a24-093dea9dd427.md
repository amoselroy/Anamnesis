WORLD PATTERN 2026-08-26 — Bot-protection intermittency in manual testing differing from production pathway — 2026-08-26

PRINCIPLE: An infrastructure block that appears reliably during manual testing (hitting individual ticket detail pages) may be phantom in production pathways (which use aggregated listing pages or LLM shortcuts), creating false impression of systematic fragility.

NARRATIVE: White Eagle Hall SeeTickets events appeared unreachable when three stub rows pointed to individual event detail URLs and showed "This event has ended" or bot-challenge blocks. Investigating those stub rows as production pipeline failures suggested a systemic weakness. However, the actual production pipeline for White Eagle Hall never fetches individual SeeTickets detail pages at all; it uses a calendar page LLM shortcut that has been reliably productive for two months. The bot-block only manifests when hitting ticket URLs by hand. The lesson: manual testing of single-use URLs (stub rows, experimental links) can trigger defensive infrastructure that the production system's actual pathway avoids entirely. Treating manual-testing failures as production bugs creates wasted remediation effort on phantom problems.
