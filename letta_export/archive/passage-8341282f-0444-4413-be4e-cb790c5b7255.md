SUMMARY
A parallel exhibition_scraper.py was built to handle the "Exhibitions Pending" sheet (16 existing rows with a different schema: start/end date ranges instead of specific datetimes, "Permanent" flag, periodic re-posting of active shows). The exhibition scraper uses a similar 5-path architecture but optimizes for multi-day/open-ended shows rather than timed events, focusing on extracting start and end dates using Haiku LLM. Initial testing targeted four exhibition sources (Barsky Gallery, Smush Gallery, Mana Contemporary, Liberty Science Center) and discovered that Hoboken Historical Museum's Squarespace site required special handling: Playwright's `load` strategy fired too early before JS execution completed, but `networkidle` + 30s timeout successfully rendered the full page. A subsequent issue arose where HTML truncation at 80,000 characters was cutting off exhibition content on Squarespace sites; this was increased to accommodate the larger HTML trees that Squarespace generates. Testing with the corrected scraper successfully extracted 23 new exhibitions across four sources: 12 from Liberty Science Center (permanent exhibits including Thomas & Friends), 2 from Hoboken Historical Museum ("What They Saw: Hoboken's Amateur Photography Clubs 1889-1934" and "Blooming Hoboken: New Paintings by Bill Curran"), and 9 from Mana Contemporary with proper date range extraction (e.g., "ECHOES" May 17 → Aug 28, "OPEN BOOK(S)" Apr 3 → Sep 1). The exhibition scraper was scheduled for weekly execution (vs. nightly for events) since exhibitions change slowly.
