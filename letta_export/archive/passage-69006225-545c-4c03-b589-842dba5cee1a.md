WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-19 — LLM relevance filter as post-scrape quality gate in news pipelines — 2026-06-19

PRINCIPLE: After scraping all candidate articles and applying simple whitelist filtering, a second-stage LLM relevance check can dramatically reduce noise by rejecting articles that only incidentally mention the therapy.

NARRATIVE: After news scraping for individual therapies, Amos observed that many results were irrelevant — articles about cooking Rosemary, general condition news without therapy content, or articles mentioning the therapy only in passing. A strict LLM relevance filter was added to the pipeline: after scraping and outlet whitelisting, all titles are passed to Haiku with the therapy name, synonyms, and condition as context. The prompt is deliberately strict: "keep only articles specifically focused on this therapy as an intervention for this condition." This two-stage filtering (outlet whitelist → LLM relevance) is more effective than a single broad filter because it preserves legitimate edge cases while catching semantic noise. The pattern applies wherever you have heterogeneous source data (news from many outlets) and need to ensure relevance to a specific narrow topic.
