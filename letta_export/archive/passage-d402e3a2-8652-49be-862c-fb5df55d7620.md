SUMMARY
The brokerage_sharer automation had been successfully scraping the Exit on the Hudson Realty page and classifying posts, but was failing at the final step: clicking the Share button. Over multiple iterations, the root cause was narrowed from a missing button (it was visually present) to a selector mismatch, then to a DOM structure problem.

**Initial findings:** Direct `aria-label` and CSS selectors found no matching buttons. A screenshot showed the Share button (→ icon with count "2") was present in the post action bar but invisible to Playwright's selector-based search.

**DOM inspection:** The accessibility tree revealed the Share button element was `role="generic"` (not `role="button"`), was not keyboard-focusable, and existed as a plain `<i>` icon inside plain `<div>` wrappers with no semantic button roles anywhere in its chain. This made standard `role="button"` selectors ineffective and Tab navigation impossible.

**Breakthrough discovery:** A full button list dump revealed the actual `aria-label` of the functional share trigger: **"Send this to friends or post it on your profile."** — not the "Share" label that other selectors were targeting. This exact label appeared in the accessibility tree and was the correct button to click.

**Multiple approaches attempted and abandoned:** Early attempts used JavaScript to walk up the DOM from a Like button to find a parent container with 3 children (Like, Comment, Share), but the Like button was frequently not found or was inside a different element structure. Coordinate-based clicking (1088, 396) hit the page sidebar instead of the action bar.

**Final working solution:** Using `target.scroll_into_view_if_needed()` to ensure the article is visible, then searching at page level for the button with `aria-label="Send this to friends or post it on your profile."`, and clicking the one closest to the article's center position. This approach found the Share button reliably on every run.
