WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-11 — Color contrast failures in shadows against similarly-hued backgrounds — 2026-06-11

PRINCIPLE: When applying drop shadows to UI elements, color-tinting the shadow to match the element's hue will make the shadow invisible if placed on a similarly-hued background.

NARRATIVE: During refinement of the Active Trials callout box, the initial implementation used a blue-tinted shadow (`rgba(0,100,180,0.15)`) to give the cards a "cohesive" appearance with the blue background. When rendered on the `#f0f9ff` light blue background, the shadow was nearly invisible — both the element and the shadow blended into the same color space. The fix was to switch to a neutral dark shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.10)`) that provides genuine contrast regardless of background color. This is a specific instance of a broader principle: shadows convey depth through contrast, and that contrast is lost when shadow and background share the same hue. The error was compounded by iteration cycles where the original blue tint was justified as "thematic," making the invisibility harder to diagnose than it would have been if the intent had been pure contrast.
