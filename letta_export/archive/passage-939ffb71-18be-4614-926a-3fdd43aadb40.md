WORLD PATTERN 2026-08-06 — Title as structural commitment to narrative arc — 2026-08-06

PRINCIPLE: A chapter title should name the genuine arc of the content, not just its subject matter; when a proposed title captures only the disruption without the resolution, it misrepresents the actual trajectory.

NARRATIVE: Initial title proposal for Chapter Three was "Topsy Turvy — The nature of our interpretation," which captured the disruption and reframing well but left out the arc's ending (settled peace, held equilibrium, not disorder). The final title "Towards a new equilibrium — The nature of our evolution" succeeded by naming the *trajectory*: the "towards" acknowledging incompleteness and directionality, "our" emphasizing collaboration as constitutive, and "evolution" naming genuine development rather than repair or return. The principle: a title that names only the conflict or starting state misleads about what the chapter accomplishes; titling should commit to reflecting the actual arc.
