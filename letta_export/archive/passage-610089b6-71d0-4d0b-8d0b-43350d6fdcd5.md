SUMMARY
Amos raised a critical discovery during curation testing: sources are currently scoped to a therapy only (each source has a therapy_id foreign key), but they should be scoped to a therapy-condition pair. The use case is that an article about Acetyl L-Carnitine treating Parkinson's is a different article from one about Acetyl L-Carnitine treating Alzheimer's disease, even though they mention the same drug. Under the current architecture, removing the Parkinson's article from the Alzheimer's junction would still hide it globally under Acetyl L-Carnitine, making it impossible to attach the same URL to a different condition where it's actually relevant. The root cause is a schema mismatch: news articles are fetched and curated at the condition level (update_news iterates per-condition), but stored at the therapy level. The proposed fix is structural: add condition_id (nullable FK) to the sources table, making each news article belong to a therapy-condition pair. This requires: (1) schema change with backward compatibility (existing articles stay condition_id = NULL); (2) update_news would store condition_id on each inserted row; (3) public display would filter by active condition or NULL (compat); (4) Manage News becomes condition-scoped (button moves to condition-specific context); (5) dedup becomes (therapy_id, condition_id, url) so the same URL can intentionally exist under different conditions. Amos confirmed this is a problem requiring the architectural refactor before proceeding further with news curation workflows. The decision was made to build this properly rather than workaround it.
