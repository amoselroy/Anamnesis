SUMMARY
Designed and implemented automatic synonym discovery: when a therapy is first researched and has no aliases yet, an AI call asks Claude to generate common PubMed search terms for that therapy, then those aliases are automatically persisted to `therapy_aliases` before the scrape runs. On subsequent research runs, the aliases are already stored so the AI call is skipped and the cached terms are used directly. This allows the scraper to find literature under the names the research community actually uses (e.g., "sauna bathing" and "Finnish sauna" for Sauna) without requiring curators to manually enter synonyms. The auto-generated aliases are visible and editable in the admin panel, allowing curators to review, modify, or supplement them. For Sauna specifically, the system generated five synonyms (Finnish sauna, dry sauna, heat therapy, sauna bathing, whole-body hyperthermia) and PubMed returned 5 relevant papers.
