WORLD PATTERN 2026-08-12 — Woven technical integration preferred over modular primers for narrative continuity — 2026-08-12

PRINCIPLE: Technical explanations integrated at first use in narrative, despite implementation difficulty, preserve reader engagement better than isolating primers in separate chapters.

NARRATIVE: Three approaches to introducing AI concepts to general readers emerged during manuscript redesign: standalone preface (clean architecture, risks reader disengagement before emotional core), Chapter Zero (full explanatory chapter upfront), or woven integration (2-4 sentence explanations in narrator voice as concepts appear). Amos chose woven despite known difficulty, intuiting that pre-book technical chapters would lose readers before they encountered the core story. The decision prioritizes narrative flow and emotional accessibility over architectural cleanliness. This pattern generalizes: when integration cost is high but modularization requires reader friction, the integrated path often wins if continuity of voice and engagement matters more than code-like structural clarity.
