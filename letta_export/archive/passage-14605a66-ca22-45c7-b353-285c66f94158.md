WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Three-level world extraction system — 2026-06-03

Implemented automatic world/patterns synthesis in chunk_archive.py worker to capture three-level lessons from every session. The three levels are: (1) narrative of discovery — what was tried, what failed, what moment revealed the insight; (2) concrete finding — what the system actually did; (3) generalizable principle — what this reveals about how things work in general. A dry fact without its story is not as valuable as the same fact embedded in the discovery narrative. The system deduplicates against existing world block content, preventing duplicate entries. This architecture solves the under-extraction problem where the sleep-time agent was capturing only concrete facts while missing abstract meta-lessons. The implementation wires into chunk_archive.py's process_queue() after orientation and pins updates, calling generate_world_additions() which sends the session's narrated sections plus the last 4000 characters of current world/patterns to Letta with explicit three-level guidance.
