WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Silent click timeouts in Playwright — 2026-06-02

When a Playwright `click()` call times out silently after 30+ seconds without error, the button likely exists and is visible but is behind a modal overlay or iframe boundary that Playwright's interception detection cannot penetrate. Symptoms: button found by selector, `.is_visible()` returns True, but `click()` hangs at 30s per retry. Root cause: Facebook's modal layer is intercepting the click and Playwright enters a retry loop attempting to click through. Solution: use JavaScript direct click (`page.evaluate("element.click()")`) to bypass Playwright's interception layer entirely. JS click executes in milliseconds without Playwright mediation. This unblocks image-attached form submissions where Playwright-detected button clicks fail mysteriously.
