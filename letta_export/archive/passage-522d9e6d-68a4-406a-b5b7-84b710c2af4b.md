WORLD PATTERN 2026-08-12 — Intermittent bot-protection with re-check strategy improves but doesn't guarantee resolution — 2026-08-12

PRINCIPLE: Infrastructure blocking that is genuinely intermittent cannot be resolved permanently in a single execution cycle; improve handling without claiming resolution, and mark for ongoing multi-cycle observation.

NARRATIVE: JC Downtown and Segunda Quimbamba both hit Cloudflare interstitials intermittently. Implementing a re-check strategy (wait 7 seconds, retry before cloudscraper fallback) showed both sites loading clean during testing, but the assessment was "the block appears intermittent and requires ongoing monitoring" rather than "we fixed it." This is the distinction between claiming permanent resolution and honestly tracking intermittent infrastructure: a solution that works once during testing is not verified until it works across multiple production cycles. Systems that claim to "fix" intermittent blocking create false confidence; epistemic honesty requires acknowledging that intermittency requires observation across multiple scheduled runs before any confidence claim is warranted.
