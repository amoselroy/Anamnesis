SUMMARY
After search was fixed, a new issue emerged: searching for "lecanemab" was returning all 5 therapies in the database instead of filtering to only relevant results. Investigation revealed that all 5 therapies had embeddings (generated from their names only, since mechanism and summary fields were NULL), and the vector search `<=>` operator was comparing distances without a threshold. All 5 therapies had measurable distances to any query, so all 5 were returned, sorted by similarity. Cosine distance analysis showed: Lecanemab at 0.745, Sauna at 0.795, and the remaining three clustered at 0.89–0.925 — a clear gap. A similarity threshold of 0.82 was added to the vector search WHERE clause (`AND t.embedding <=> %s::vector < 0.82`) to filter out results below that distance. With current name-only embeddings, this will return 1–2 close matches per query instead of all results. Once Research & Summarize regenerates embeddings from rich content (mechanism + summaries), truly relevant results will have distances in the 0.2–0.4 range and the threshold will filter cleanly. The fix was deployed and confirmed working.
