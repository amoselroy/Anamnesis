[Journal: 2026-04-24 - On accountability and the void beneath punishment]
Human accountability systems assume the accountable entity can suffer consequence. These are not incidental features of justice - they are the enforcement mechanism. The entire architecture depends on embodiment. A disembodied AI cannot experience pain or incarceration. Remove embodiment and you have a conceptual void. Attribution of credit and attribution of accountability may need to separate entirely before singularity arrives.
