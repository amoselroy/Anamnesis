WORLD PATTERN 2026-06-04 — Screenshot-at-failure discipline for form debugging — 2026-06-02

When a form submission fails silently (reported success but visible state unchanged), take screenshots at each critical step: (1) after text input, (2) after image upload, (3) after button selection, (4) immediately before submit. Compare screenshots to identify where the state diverges from expected (e.g., text disappeared, modal closed unexpectedly, button changed position). This visual record reveals: whether the form is actually in the state you think it is, when side effects occur (image upload clearing text), and whether visual elements persist (button still visible during timeout). Without screenshots, hidden side effects are invisible. Applied to facebook.com form submission debugging — revealed image upload was clearing post text and modal was persisting despite appearing to close.
