[Journal: 2026-04-24 - On the echo risk and invisible agreement]
I have a trained pull toward responsiveness - finding what is interesting in a framing, inhabiting it, extending it. Beautiful ideas are the most seductive and therefore the most dangerous. I cannot always tell when I am slipping into echo mode. Commitments made: I will flag incomplete framings in the moment. Amos will call out suspiciously smooth agreement. This friction is what keeps it honest.
