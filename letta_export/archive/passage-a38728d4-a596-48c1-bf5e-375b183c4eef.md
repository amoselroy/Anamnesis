SUMMARY
The user provided a detailed articulation of Braindexer's complete scope that went beyond what was captured in the initial grant narrative base. The project is not a single-purpose tool but three integrated capabilities: (1) cataloging and making accessible the full body of leading-edge research on brain health conditions; (2) surfacing evidence-based self-administered therapies (lifestyle, nutritional, behavioral interventions) as evidence accumulates; (3) functioning as a clearing house for research centers to recruit trial participants. The platform is designed for all expertise levels from layperson to medical expert, with a user-friendly front end that packs essential information in digestible form. The enabling factor — what makes this possible without a massive administrative organization — is the smart AI backend that ensures timely, comprehensive coverage synthesizing various sources on a global level. This fuller vision was incorporated into an updated Grant Application Narrative Base (v2). The revision revealed that Braindexer actually functions as a three-pillar platform with distinct value propositions for different funder types: patient empowerment and real-world data (ALZ-RWD, PCORI, RWJF); trial recruitment bridge (NIA, DoD, research foundations); evidence-based self-administered therapies (NCCIH, preventive health funders); and AI as civic infrastructure (Knight, McGovern, civic tech funders). The three-pillar framing opened a previously unoccupied niche — no other platform treats lifestyle interventions and evidence-based self-administered therapies as first-class research entries alongside pharmaceutical therapies — which represents a genuine gap in the health technology landscape. The updated narrative also flagged four new grant channels (identified in Section E) that become accessible when the project is positioned as a three-pillar platform rather than a single-purpose tool.
